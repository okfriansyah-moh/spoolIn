# SpoolIn brand history

Adopted on 11 October 2026, Asia/Jakarta.

## Name and origin

**SpoolIn** takes its name from **the spool that holds a length of film**. Write the brand as SpoolIn, with uppercase S and I, in headings, prose, wordmarks, and interface titles. Use `spoolin` for filesystem slugs.

## The story behind the name

SpoolIn takes its name from the spool that holds a length of film. Unspooling reveals a story frame by frame; SpoolIn carries that idea into discovering films, series, and the people behind them. “In” invites exploration, moving from a title into the stories and connections behind it. Every reel holds a story.

Film spools are part of the physical history of cinema; the [Science Museum Group’s film-reel collection](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/search/objects/object_type/film-reel) includes spools used with film projectors.

## Brand promise and homepage copy

**Brand promise and homepage supporting copy:** Unspooling stories and discovering what’s behind them.

**Homepage headline:** Every reel holds a story.

For SpoolIn, discovery moves from a movie or series into its metadata, cast, creators, and credits. The copy reflects the approved catalog experience and gives its people and connections a place alongside its artwork.

## Visual identity and voice

Keep the adopted paired-frame symbol, Cinema at night dark theme, Film gallery light theme, typography, and artwork. The spool origin informs the meaning of the name; it does not require a new logo or theme. Use the expressive headline on the discovery homepage and clear, direct language for search, forms, errors, and catalog management.
