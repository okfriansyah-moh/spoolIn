# TV poster sourcing research

Research date: 18 October 2026. This is a bounded source audit, not proof that additional qualifying posters do not exist.

## Result

**0 fully approved title/poster pairs in this pass.** Approval requires identity, rights, and visual confirmation together. Metadata produced a useful candidate inventory, but no 100-title package was established. The exact acceptance shortfall is therefore **100 approved TV pairs**; this is a verification shortfall, not a claim of global unavailability.

Live Commons category query returned **28 files and one subcategory**. The files include duplicates, square artwork, logos, photographs, and unrelated material. A category count is not a count of usable posters. Four further candidates were found outside that category.

The strict portrait candidate pool below has 12 distinct works with permission indicators in the category plus four external leads; these remain unapproved. No seed or app files were changed.

## Search breadth and limitations

Searches covered Commons television/series-poster categories, broader poster/series/VRT searches, public-domain TV searches, and official broadcaster/open-license searches. The parent Television posters category supplied two additional leads, not a large reusable collection. Commons logos and public-domain screenshots were deliberately excluded. No official broadcaster collection with 100 eligible portrait posters was established.

Initial web image preview clicks returned tool errors. A later source-page browser inspection displayed the Leap Year portrait poster with a readable title, and confirmed the page's creator and VRT permission notices. This resolves visual availability for that candidate only; its first-air date and complete package evidence remain unapproved. No image bytes were downloaded to work around display restrictions. Other metadata-only candidates were not promoted to approved artwork. BBC browsing was blocked by robots restrictions; no claim about unavailable BBC licenses is made.

The Koza subcategory and other targeted leads may supply further candidates, but cannot bridge the acceptance gap without individual review. Repeated posters, seasons, episodes, square crops, logos, and title cards must not inflate the distinct series count.

## Recommendation

Retain the current seed until 100 movie pairs and 100 TV pairs pass the agreed research gate. Continue targeted sourcing or seek rights-holder permission if further time is available. A smaller verified catalog or broader licensed source would require a user decision; neither was substituted here.
